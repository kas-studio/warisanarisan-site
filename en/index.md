# Warisan vs Arisan: Merge Kafe - Indonesian Culinary Merge & Estate Mystery

> **Warisan vs Arisan: Merge Kafe** (https://warisanarisan.com/en) is an offline-friendly Indonesian culinary merge puzzle and architectural restoration mystery game for **iOS and Android** (`id.griyacempaka.warisanarisan`), developed by **KAS Studio** in South Jakarta, Indonesia (https://kasstudio.id).

## Core Highlights
- **36 Authentic Indonesian Dishes & Keepsakes** across 4 chains on a calming 7x9 (63-cell) Celadon-Jade portrait board.
- **12 Griya Cempaka Rooms x 3 Indonesian Architectural Styles** (switchable anytime without re-purchasing).
- **12 Wax-Sealed Amplop Wasiat Mystery Chapters** filled with warm Indonesian family & Arisan society drama.
- **Player-Friendly Offline Design**: 4x25 daily bonus energy refills preserved permanently, zero forced interstitial interruptions, playable offline.
